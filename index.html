Revision Septorhinoplasty: An Updated Narrative Review of Current Concepts in Evaluation and Surgical Management

Facial Plastic Surgery · PMID 43044676 · doi

事实｜翻修鼻综述覆盖7类畸形，主张先重建结构、再精修外形，全文无新原始数据。

点评｜叙述性综述，作者自己承认缺高质量翻修研究——当操作清单可以，别当证据等级。

This narrative review covers seven revision deformity patterns and argues that stable structural reconstruction should precede contour refinement; it reports no new primary data. A narrative review whose own authors call for higher-quality revision-specific studies: a checklist, not proof of superiority.

鼻整形肋软骨系统综述

Adverse Outcomes and Complications of Autologous Versus Homologous Costal Cartilage Grafts in Septorhinoplasty: A Systematic Review and Meta-analysis

Aesthetic Surgery Journal · PMID 42374829 · doi

事实｜50项研究4482例：自体肋与同种肋并发症无显著差异，总翘曲2.9%、感染1.9%、翻修5.0%。

点评｜偏倚严重到critical、有小样本效应，选材按可得性和成本，别按并发症差异讲。

Across 50 studies and 4482 patients, autologous, irradiated, Tutoplast and fresh-frozen costal cartilage showed no significant difference in warping, infection, extrusion or revision. Risk of bias was serious to critical with small-study effects, so graft choice should follow availability and cost rather than claimed safety differences.

鼻整形患者报告结局系统综述

Comprehensive Evaluation of Quality of Life in Patients After Rhinoplasty Using Patient-Reported Outcome Measures (PROMs): A Systematic Review

Plastic and Aesthetic Nursing · PMID 42895916 · doi

事实｜28项研究：鼻整形后功能、外观与心理评分方向一致改善，无某术式占优，MCID报告少。

点评｜设计杂、多数没报MCID，能说的是多数人自觉好转，报不出具体涨几分。

A 28-study systematic review found consistent improvements across NOSE, SNOT-22, SCHNOS, ROE and FACE-Q after rhinoplasty, with no technique superior and limited MCID reporting. Uniform direction of effect across heterogeneous designs still does not let you promise a specific score gain.

拉皮人工智能疗效评估

Comparing Deep Plane and Superficial Musculoaponeurotic System Facelifts Using Artificial Intelligence-Based Age Estimation

Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery – Global Open · PMID 42775335 · doi

事实｜200例照片经AI测龄：正面仅减2.5–2.8岁，侧面误差16–23岁，两种术式无差异。

点评｜照片取自公开网站、无随访，AI连侧面都测不准，证明不了哪种拉皮更好。

In 200 photographs, AI estimated only 2.5-2.8 years of apparent age reduction frontally, with 16-23 years mean absolute error in profile, and no difference between deep plane and SMAS. Web-sourced photos without follow-up: AI age estimation cannot adjudicate which facelift technique works better.

拉皮围手术期安全

Predictors of Unplanned Admission After Outpatient Rhytidectomy

Aesthetic Surgery Journal · PMID 42322187 · doi

事实｜129例门诊拉皮16.3%非计划住院，与麻醉574对486分钟、合并术式多、肥胖和高血压相关。

点评｜单中心回顾129例、四级证据；能用来排台，不能当预测模型，合并项目越多越要留床位。

Of 129 outpatient rhytidectomies, 16.3% required unplanned admission, associated with longer anesthesia (574.6 vs 485.7 min), more concurrent procedures, obesity and hypertension. Level-4 single-center data: usable for scheduling and bed planning, not a validated prediction model.

隆胸包膜挛缩影像评估

Shear-Wave Elastography for Predicting the Occurrence and Assessing the Severity of Capsular Contracture After Implant-Based Breast Augmentation and Reconstruction: A Review of the Evidence

Journal of Clinical Medicine · PMID 43030552 · doi

事实｜8项研究（每项2–51例）：剪切波弹性成像测包膜硬度随Baker分级升高，阈值未外部验证。

点评｜样本小、无前瞻预测研究，SWE只能当触诊的补充，不能凭一个kPa数决定手术。

Eight reports of 2-51 patients each found periprosthetic stiffness rising with Baker grade, but neither proposed threshold (~52 and 82 kPa) has been externally validated. No study shows early elastography predicts later contracture; it remains an adjunct to palpation, not a surgical indication.
