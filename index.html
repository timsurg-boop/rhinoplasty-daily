鼻整形肋软骨患者报告结局

Patient-Reported Functional and Cosmetic Outcomes after Rhinoplasty with Autologous Versus Cadaveric Rib Cartilage Grafts

Facial Plastic Surgery & Aesthetic Medicine · PMID 43035971 · doi

事实｜129例鼻整形，自体肋33例、异体肋96例；随诊鼻塞评分自体组低14.0分，外形无差异。

点评｜自体肋换来的是通气不是外形；翻修率15.2%对24.0%区间跨零，别当优势讲。

In 129 adults (33 autologous, 96 cadaveric rib grafts), autologous recipients averaged obstruction scores 14.0 points lower over follow-up (95% CI -24.2 to -3.9), with no significant difference in cosmetic scores or revisions (15.2% vs 24.0%). Autologous rib bought better breathing, not a better-looking nose, and the revision gap is too imprecise to sell as an advantage.

鼻整形神经阻滞RCT

Infraorbital versus sphenopalatine ganglion block for patient-reported recovery after septorhinoplasty: A randomized controlled trial

Journal of Clinical Anesthesia · PMID 42950360 · doi

事实｜90例鼻中隔鼻整形RCT：眶下神经阻滞24小时恢复质量129.1分，蝶腭节阻滞116.6分。

点评｜12.5分超过8分最小临床意义，补救镇痛11.4%对53.3%；单中心，等重复。

In a 90-patient randomized trial, infraorbital nerve block produced a 24-hour QoR-15 of 129.1 versus 116.6 for sphenopalatine ganglion block, with rescue analgesia in 11.4% versus 53.3%. The 12.5-point gap exceeds the 8-point minimal important difference and halves rescue analgesia, but it is one centre's result awaiting replication.

鼻整形注射新技术

“Dou-gong” Technique: A Novel Biomechanical Approach to Injection Rhinoplasty Based on Nasal Ligament Anatomy

Aesthetic Plastic Surgery · PMID 42897251 · doi

事实｜100例注射鼻整形：27G针在骨膜/软骨膜上按五点支撑鼻韧带，无血管栓塞或移位。

点评｜把填充从堆容量改成撑韧带，思路新；无对照、无客观测量，只能算可行。

One hundred injection-rhinoplasty patients were treated with a 27-gauge needle above periosteum or perichondrium at five nasal zones to reinforce ligament support, with no vascular occlusion or filler displacement. Reframing filler as ligament support rather than volume is a real idea, but with no control group and no objective measurement it only shows feasibility.

拉皮麻醉并发症

SMAS Plication Facelift and Platysmaplasty Under Tumescent Local Anesthesia: a 10-Year Retrospective Evaluation of Safety, Efficacy, and Patient Satisfaction in an Outpatient Setting

Aesthetic Plastic Surgery · PMID 42791356 · doi

事实｜80例SMAS折叠拉皮加颈阔肌成形在肿胀局麻下完成，10年随诊并发症10%，无全麻转归。

点评｜血肿2.5%、血清肿1.25%，比全麻组不差；但这是筛选过的ASA I-II、单术者。

Eighty ASA I-II patients underwent SMAS plication facelift with platysmaplasty under tumescent local anesthesia over ten years, with a 10% complication rate (hematoma 2.5%) and no conversions to general anesthesia. Those complication rates are respectable, but this is a single-surgeon series in selected ASA I-II patients, not a comparison against general anesthesia.

隆胸经脐入路系统综述

The Transumbilical Breast Augmentation: Safety and Satisfaction in a Systematic Review and Meta-analysis of 13,210 Breasts

Aesthetic Plastic Surgery · PMID 42993310 · doi

事实｜17项研究、6605例（13210侧）经脐隆胸：血肿0.2%、包膜挛缩1.7%、再干预2.3%。

点评｜数据低得好看，但几乎都是单臂回顾、随访短，作者自己说不能证明等效。

A meta-analysis of 17 studies and 6,605 patients (13,210 breasts) pooled transumbilical augmentation to 0.2% hematoma, 1.7% capsular contracture and 2.3% reintervention. Almost all included series were retrospective single-arm studies with short follow-up, so these low rates describe published experience rather than prove equivalence to standard incisions.

隆胸层次选择Meta 分析

Clinical Outcomes of Submuscular vs Subglandular Implant Placement in Primary Cosmetic Breast Augmentation: A Systematic Review and Meta-analysis

Aesthetic Surgery Journal · PMID 42969680 · doi

事实｜12项研究、4534侧：肌下/双平面总体并发症OR 0.49，包膜挛缩0.58，血肿0.30。

点评｜并发症更少，满意度和再手术没差别；层次还是按软组织厚度和动画畸形定。

Pooled data from 12 studies (4,534 breasts) showed submuscular or dual-plane placement had lower odds of overall complications (OR 0.49), capsular contracture (0.58) and hematoma (0.30), with no difference in satisfaction or reoperation. Fewer complications does not mean a better-looking breast; pocket choice still follows tissue coverage, animation deformity and the individual patient.
