鼻整形人工智能医患沟通

Detecting Manipulated Online Rhinoplasty Images Using an Artificial Intelligence Facial Authenticity Localization (FAL) Model

Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery · PMID 42765934 · doi

事实｜抓取 RealSelf 公开图库连续 600 张鼻整形术后照，用 FAL 模型检测像素级真伪：19.5%（117/600）存在几何篡改。200 张原始临床照假阳性 1.5%，50 张 Facetune 变形照全部检出，敏感度 100%。

点评｜创新点：AI 真伪检测首次在整形外科图片上大规模跑，把“照片修过”从印象变成 19.5% 这个可核的数。意义：面诊时患者拿来当模板的参考图，可以先过一遍检测，再谈能不能做。

The Facial Authenticity Localization model was applied to the first 600 consecutive postoperative rhinoplasty photos in RealSelf's public gallery, flagging geometric manipulation in 19.5% (117/600; 95% CI 16.3-22.7%). Specificity testing on 200 presumed-unedited clinical photos gave a 1.5% false-positive rate, and all 50 Facetune-warped images were detected (sensitivity 100%). This is the first large-scale authenticity audit of aesthetic-surgery media, converting 'that photo is edited' into a measurable 19.5%. It offers a defensible pre-consultation check on the reference images patients bring, though validation covered only Facetune-style warps.

鼻整形保鼻背鼻腔通气

Nasal Airflow Changes in Dorsal Preservation Rhinoplasty: A Computational Fluid Dynamics Analysis in Human Anatomic Specimens

Facial Plastic Surgery & Aesthetic Medicine · PMID 42806791 · doi

事实｜4 具新鲜人体解剖标本，锥形束 CT 建模后做计算流体力学模拟，比较鼻中隔成形与保鼻背鼻整形的气流：除 1 例外，术后鼻阻力均下降，变化方向随上外侧软骨在内鼻阀顶端的朝向而不同。

点评｜创新点：保鼻背对通气的影响，此前只有小样本量表和鼻阻力计、结论互相打架；这篇首次在真实解剖标本上用流体力学算出阻力变化。意义：不必预设保鼻背会堵，但差异取决于上外侧软骨朝向。

Computational fluid dynamics was run on cone-beam CT models of four fresh human anatomic specimens before and after septoplasty and dorsal preservation rhinoplasty. Nasal resistance decreased in every specimen but one, and the direction of change tracked the orientation of the upper lateral cartilage at the apical internal nasal valve. Earlier evidence on preservation and airflow came from small rhinomanometry and questionnaire samples that disagreed; this is the first CFD quantification in real anatomic specimens. It argues against assuming preservation stiffens the airway, but four specimens establish direction, not a magnitude to quote in consent.

拉皮深层平面系统综述

Deep Plane Facelifts: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of Outcomes

Aesthetic Plastic Surgery · PMID 42576065 · doi

事实｜45 项研究、10,784 例深层平面拉皮的系统综述与 meta 分析：总体并发症 6.6%，无永久性面神经损伤，暂时性面神经损伤 1.2%、血肿 1.8%、血清肿 1.7%、感染 0.8%、增生性瘢痕 2.8%、皮肤坏死 0.1%。

点评｜创新点：把“深层平面容易伤面神经”这个流传几十年的顾虑，用一万例合并率压到零例永久损伤。意义：面诊不必再拿神经风险劝人改做 SMAS，该谈的是血肿 1.8%、瘢痕 2.8%；外观更优仍是主观评价。

A PRISMA systematic review and meta-analysis pooled 45 studies and 10,784 deep plane facelifts: overall complications were 6.6%, with no permanent facial nerve injury and temporary nerve injury at 1.2%. Haematoma occurred in 1.8%, seroma 1.7%, infection 0.8%, hypertrophic scarring 2.8% and skin necrosis 0.1%. It retires the decades-old fear that deep plane dissection costs facial nerve function, with zero permanent injuries across more than ten thousand cases. Consent can shift to haematoma 1.8% and scarring 2.8%; the claimed aesthetic superiority over SMAS remains a subjective judgement.
