鼻整形循证网络meta分析

Perioperative Interventions to Reduce Edema and Ecchymosis After Rhinoplasty: A Network Meta-Analysis of Randomized Controlled Trials

Aesthetic Surgery Journal · PMID 43017812 · doi

事实｜36项RCT网络meta：激素消肿退瘀最稳；氨甲环酸只对早期瘀斑有效（SMD -0.80）。

点评｜激素是唯一稳定的；激素+氨甲环酸的“最佳组合”证据等级低，只能算假设，别当定论。

In a network meta-analysis of 36 randomized trials, corticosteroids consistently reduced post-rhinoplasty edema and ecchymosis, while tranexamic acid only modestly reduced early ecchymosis (SMD -0.80). Corticosteroids were the only consistently supported agent; the corticosteroid-plus-TXA combination ranked highest on SUCRA but rests on sparse indirect evidence rated low to very low certainty.

Iatrogenic pseudoaneurysm formation in the medial orbitofrontal artery following rhinoplasty and nasal osteotomy: A case report

Surgical Neurology International · PMID 42974243 · doi

事实｜31岁男鼻整形截骨后颅内出血，造影见内侧眶额动脉假性动脉瘤，显微夹闭后无后遗症。

点评｜截骨后头痛、呕吐、意识变化别只当麻醉反应；尽早血管造影，罕见但致命。

A 31-year-old man developed intracerebral hemorrhage after rhinoplasty with osteotomy; angiography showed a medial orbitofrontal artery pseudoaneurysm, treated by microsurgical trapping and clipping with complete neurological recovery. Intracranial pseudoaneurysm after osteotomy is rare but catastrophic, so post-rhinoplasty hemorrhage and neuro symptoms warrant prompt vascular imaging instead of watchful waiting.

拉皮人工智能FACE-Q

Quantifying Facial Rejuvenation after Deep Plane Facelift Using Convolutional Neural Network-Based Age Estimation: Correlation with Patient-Reported Outcomes on the FACE-Q Aesthetic Module

Aesthetic Plastic Surgery · PMID 42897248 · doi

事实｜72例深层除皱，5个AI模型估龄降7.64岁（15.7%），与FACE-Q弱相关（ρ 0.25）。

点评｜AI估龄可当客观指标，但病人自评年轻多少才是主结局；两者的数别混着报。

In 72 deep-plane face and neck lifts, five CNN models estimated a mean age reduction of 7.64 years (15.7%), but AI estimates correlated only weakly with FACE-Q scores (rho 0.25). AI age estimation is reproducible as an objective metric, yet patient-perceived rejuvenation correlated far more strongly (rho 0.61) and should stay the primary outcome.

隆胸包膜挛缩翻修

The Distal U-Shaped Capsule Sparing Technique (DUS-CS) for Revision Breast Augmentation: A Consecutive Clinical Series of 223 Patients

Aesthetic Plastic Surgery · PMID 42881301 · doi

事实｜223例包膜挛缩翻修用U形保留包膜技术，现有随访内无复发、无双乳相通、无下移。

点评｜留部分包膜能稳住腔隙、少创伤；但这是单中心描述性研究，随访时长没写，别当治愈率。

In 223 consecutive revision augmentations, a U-shaped selective capsulectomy sparing medial, lateral and caudal capsule produced no recurrence of contracture, symmastia or bottoming out during available follow-up. Capsule-sparing revision preserves pocket geometry with less morbidity, but this single-center descriptive series does not report follow-up duration and cannot establish a recurrence rate.

隆胸下皱襞缝合技术

A Modified Pre-implantation Internal Fixation Suture Technique for Inframammary Fold Reconstruction in Implant-Based Breast Augmentation: A Retrospective Cohort Study

Aesthetic Plastic Surgery · PMID 42820993 · doi

事实｜53例下皱襞入路用改良内固定缝法，3例假体下移<5mm，无翻修，平均随访12个月。

点评｜技术可行，但“满意度100%”出自单术者回顾、无对照；假体位置得看更长期。

In 53 IMF-approach augmentations using a modified internal fixation suture, 3 implants displaced less than 5 mm inferiorly, none needed revision, and mean follow-up was 12 months. The technique is feasible for fold support, but a single-surgeon retrospective series claiming 100% satisfaction cannot establish superiority over standard closure.
