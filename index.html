Balancing Function and Aesthetics: Clinical Insights from Contemporary Rhinoplasty in a Regional Middle Eastern Cohort

Aesthetic Plastic Surgery · PMID 43039986 · doi

事实｜263例中东初次鼻整形，ROE从31.5升到84.8分，报告零重大并发症、零翻修。

点评｜满意度涨53分好看，但随访期零翻修不合常理，更像随访短或失访，别照抄这组数。

In 263 primary rhinoplasties by a single surgeon, ROE improved from 31.5 to 84.8, with no major complications or revisions reported. A 53-point ROE gain alongside zero revisions over follow-up points to short or incomplete follow-up rather than surgical superiority.

The L-Shaped Lateral Crural Graft: A Single-Graft Technique for the Correction of Alar Concavity and Nasal Valve Insufficiency

Journal of Craniofacial Surgery · PMID 42853803 · doi

事实｜12例翻修鼻用肋软骨L形外侧脚支架，随访9.5个月，无感染、外露、变形。

点评｜12例、无对照、无客观通气测量，“全部满意”只能算可行性信号，不能算疗效。

Twelve revision patients received bilateral L-shaped costal cartilage lateral crural grafts; at a mean 9.5 months there were no infections, exposures, warping or recurrences. Twelve uncontrolled cases judged by surgeon and patient report show feasibility only, not efficacy.

High vs Low Septal Preservation Rhinoplasty: A Quantitative Morphologic Analysis of Hump-Supratip Dynamics

Aesthetic Plastic Surgery · PMID 42809129 · doi

事实｜30例保留鼻整形照片测量：高鼻中隔组鼻尖上区下降更多，低鼻中隔组相对更高。

点评｜样本30、非随机、随访短；能说两种做法鼻尖上区反应不同，不能说哪个更好。

In 30 preservation rhinoplasties measured on standardized photographs, the high septal technique lowered the supratip more while the low septal approach left it relatively higher. Thirty non-randomized cases with short follow-up describe how the two variants move the supratip, not which one is better.

拉皮深平面保留式3D测量

The Next Step in Facial Aesthetic Surgery Outcome Evaluation: Quantifying Classical Deep Plane versus Preservation Facelift with 3-Dimensional Surface Imaging

Aesthetic Plastic Surgery · PMID 43039989 · doi

事实｜32例拉皮6个月3D测量：保留式中面部横向位移4.75mm，深平面2.54mm。

点评｜位移大不等于好看，分组按时间先后而非随机，耐久性作者自己也没测。

In 32 patients with 3D surface imaging at six months, the preservation facelift displaced the medial midface 4.75 mm laterally versus 2.54 mm for the deep plane lift. More displacement is not a better face, allocation followed chronology rather than randomization, and durability was not measured.

拉皮麻醉术后恶心呕吐

Postoperative nausea and vomiting after facelift surgery despite routine dual antiemetic prophylaxis

Research Square (Preprint) · PMID · doi

事实｜220例拉皮，常规双联止吐后62.3%仍恶心呕吐，49.1%真的吐了。

点评｜同期做眼睑、手术时间长是独立危险因素，Apfel评分反而没用；单中心预印本。

Despite dual antiemetic prophylaxis in 96% of 220 facelift patients, 62.3% had PONV within 24 hours and 49.1% vomited. Concurrent blepharoplasty and longer operating time independently predicted PONV while the Apfel score did not; this is a single-centre preprint.

Breast augmentation and sensory outcomes: A systematic review of surgical techniques and their impact on nipple-areola complex and breast-skin sensibility

JPRAS Open · PMID 42903738 · doi

事实｜26项研究、9190例隆胸：感觉减退多在6-12个月恢复，乳晕切口早期低感觉更多。

点评｜证据只能定性汇总、测量工具各异；可用于术前告知，不能用来给切口排名。

Across 26 studies and 9,190 patients, most sensory loss after augmentation recovered within 6 to 12 months, with more early nipple hypoesthesia after periareolar incisions. Heterogeneous measures forced a qualitative synthesis, so this informs consent rather than ranking incisions.
