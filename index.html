Anatomical Handling Properties of Costal and Auricular Cartilage in Fresh-frozen Cadaveric Models for Revision Rhinoplasty

Surgical and Radiologic Anatomy · Russo F · PMID 43062723 · doiUnit of Plastic Surgery, University of Naples "Federico II", Naples, Italy

事实｜10 具新鲜冷冻尸体头颈加 2 个胸部标本，双侧取第 5–8 肋软骨和耳廓软骨，比较切取、切薄片、缝合的手感。肋软骨硬脆似钙化，切薄片 85% 碎裂，稍厚也见微骨折、缝合时断开；耳软骨保持弹性，能稳定切薄片、缝合不断。

点评｜创新点：此前没人系统评估新鲜冷冻尸体能否模拟修复鼻整形的软骨准备。意义：在这种标本上练肋软骨切薄片，手感会练偏；练耳软骨雕刻更可靠，肋软骨只适合练开胸取骨那一段。

Ten fresh-frozen cadaveric heads and two thoracic specimens were dissected, with costal cartilage taken bilaterally from ribs 5-8 and auricular cartilage through a posterior conchal approach, then rated for graft dimensions, preparation time, carvability, laminar fragmentation and suturability. Costal cartilage behaved as rigid, brittle and calcified: laminar thinning fragmented in 85% of attempts, and thicker slices showed microfractures and broke during suturing. Auricular cartilage kept its elasticity, carved into reproducible laminas and held sutures. The study is the first systematic look at whether fresh-frozen cadavers can stand in for the cartilage work of revision rhinoplasty, and the answer is source-specific. Practising thin costal laminas on these specimens teaches the wrong feel, whereas auricular cartilage is a fair model for carving; rib harvest retains value only as thoracic-exposure training.

鼻整形唇裂鼻患者报告结局

Perception Meets Precision: Parent-Reported Outcomes of Intermediate Rhinoplasty in Unilateral Cleft Lip

Journal of Craniofacial Surgery · Bangun K · PMID 43070921 · doiDivision of Plastic Reconstructive and Aesthetic Surgery, Universitas Indonesia - Cipto Mangunkusumo Hospital, Jakarta, Indonesia

事实｜印尼唇腭裂中心 2019–2025 年的中期鼻整形患儿，28 位家长中 20 位完成 Byrne 问卷，随访 1.5 个月至 6 年。90% 满意；鼻尖突度中位分 5 升到 8.5，对称性 5 到 7，通气 8 到 9。

点评｜创新点：用经验证的问卷把家长眼中的效果量成可比的分数，随访跨度近 6 年。意义：术前谈话可以把突度、对称性大概能改善多少讲成具体档次，不只说会好看一些；但无对照、年龄跨度大，只能当参考。

Parents of children with unilateral cleft lip and palate who had intermediate rhinoplasty at Indonesia's national cleft centre between 2019 and 2025 were surveyed with the validated Byrne Questionnaire; 20 of 28 eligible parents responded (71.4%), with follow-up from 1.5 months to almost 6 years. Ninety percent reported satisfaction, and median scores rose from 5 to 8.5 for tip projection, 5 to 7 for symmetry, 5 to 7 for appearance and 8 to 9 for breathing. The innovation is quantifying the parent's view with a validated instrument across a follow-up span of nearly six years, albeit in a small single-centre sample. It lets a consultation put expected gains in projection and symmetry into concrete grades instead of vague reassurance, but the absence of a control group and the wide age range keep it at the level of reference data.

颅骨修复去骨瓣减压颅骨修补精神心理结局

Neuropsychiatric Outcomes After Decompressive Craniectomy and Cranioplasty: A Systematic Review and Meta-analysis（systematic review）

Neurosurgical Review · Bari SSM · PMID 43062567 · doiDepartment of Neurosurgery, Queen's Hospital, Barking, Havering and Redbridge University Hospitals NHS Trust, London, UK

事实｜系统综述 24 项研究、877 例去骨瓣减压患者，12 项、305 例进 Meta 分析。抑郁患病率 47%（95%CI 40–54%），焦虑 46%（38–54%）；仅 2 项纵向研究报修补后 12 个月焦虑抑郁下降。

点评｜创新点：把去骨瓣减压后抑郁焦虑的发生率合并成可比数字，样本量超过多数单中心报告。意义：这类患者随访该常规筛情绪和认知，不只盯骨窗与影像；修补术前谈话也可以提一句心理状态有改善可能。

This systematic review and meta-analysis pooled 24 studies (877 patients) after decompressive craniectomy, with 12 studies (305 patients) entering the meta-analysis. Pooled prevalence was 47% for depression (95% CI 40-54%) and 46% for anxiety (38-54%); only two longitudinal studies reported falls in both at 12 months after cranioplasty, and the authors caution that those large effect sizes may be inflated by small cohorts. The innovation is turning scattered single-centre reports into pooled prevalence figures for psychiatric morbidity after decompressive craniectomy. The practical consequence is that these patients need routine mood and cognitive screening alongside bone-window and imaging follow-up, and that pre-cranioplasty counselling can mention a likely psychological gain without promising it.
