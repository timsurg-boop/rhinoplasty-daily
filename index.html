鼻整形鼻尖支撑技术报告

Pilot Study: Preliminary Experience with Dual-strut Tip Support Using Repurposed Cephalic Trim Cartilage in East Asian Rhinoplasty

Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery – Global Open · PMID 43032422 · doi

事实｜10例日本女性初鼻，鼻中隔延伸加头侧软骨重制双柱，随诊平均10.1个月，无感染、外露。

点评｜10例、无对照，原文自称生物力学优势仍是推测；省的是第二块供区，不是手术时间。

Ten Japanese women undergoing primary rhinoplasty received a trapezoidal septal extension graft plus paired columellar struts made from repurposed cephalic-trim cartilage, with mean follow-up of 10.1 months and no infection, extrusion, septal instability or columellar widening. With 10 patients, no control group and the authors themselves calling the biomechanical advantage theoretical, this is a way to avoid a second donor site rather than a faster operation.

隆胸包膜切除综述

Capsulectomy Following Breast Implant Removal: Evidence-Based Indications and a Framework for Clinical Decision-Making

Aesthetic Plastic Surgery · PMID 42881298 · doi

事实｜综述：无研究直接比较切除与保留包膜；9590例预注册队列未见BII症状差异，切除并发症更高。

点评｜包膜切除只有四个指征：确诊ALCL、包膜恶变、有症状钙化影响钼靶、III–IV级挛缩。其余别当常规。

This review finds no study directly comparing explantation with and without capsulectomy; the only pre-registered controlled cohort (n=9590) showed no difference in BII symptom prevalence, and capsulectomy carries more complications. Capsulectomy is supported by four indications — confirmed BIA-ALCL, capsular malignancy, symptomatic calcification impairing mammography, and grade III-IV contracture — and should not be routine beyond them.

拉皮二次手术配对队列

Interval to Secondary Facelift: A Match-Cohort Analysis of Interval Time, Technique, and Complications

Aesthetic Surgery Journal · PMID 42741888 · doi

事实｜97对97配对：二次拉皮比初次短44.3分钟、更多用SMAS折叠，并发症无差异；间隔平均10.7年。

点评｜二次拉皮不必当高风险拒掉；初次越年轻间隔越长（r=-0.44）。单术者回顾性，别当承诺。

In 97 matched pairs, secondary facelifts took 44.3 minutes less than primaries and used SMAS plication more often, with complication rates no different; the mean interval was 10.7 years. A secondary facelift is not inherently high risk, and younger age at the primary operation predicted a longer interval (r=-0.44), but this is a single-surgeon retrospective cohort and not a promise.

鼻整形围手术期患者沟通

Predictors and Themes of Perioperative Patient-Initiated Communication in Septorhinoplasty

The Laryngoscope · PMID 42864859 · doi

事实｜306例鼻中隔鼻整形，64%在围手术期主动联系医疗组；术前问流程31%、问用药28%。

点评｜六成人会来问，问的是流程和用药，不是并发症；回顾性单中心，但术前须知该按这两块重写。

Among 306 septorhinoplasties, 197 patients (64%) initiated contact with the care team in the 30 days before or after surgery, with procedural details (31%) and medication questions (28%) leading preoperatively. Most calls are about logistics and medication rather than complications, so the preoperative handout should be rebuilt around those two topics, though this is a single-center retrospective review.

隆胸翻修包膜瓣

Stabilizing breast implants with the double anchor capsule flap technique: insights from a retrospective case series of 30 patients

JPRAS Open · PMID 43032105 · doi

事实｜30例双侧翻修用双锚定包膜瓣固定假体，平均减容22.3cc，1年无复发下垂、无错位、无并发症。

点评｜0并发症的30例回顾性单臂系列不能当发生率；值得学的是拿血供包膜当锚，不是那个0。

In 30 bilateral revision cases using the double-anchor capsule flap technique, implant volume was reduced by a mean of 22.3 cc and there was no recurrent ptosis, malposition or complication at one year. A single-arm series of 30 with zero complications is not an incidence rate; the transferable idea is anchoring to vascularized capsule, not the zero.

鼻整形软骨再生综述

Beyond Cartilage Grafting in Rhinoplasty: Biopharmaceutical and Biomaterial Strategies for Nasal Cartilage Regeneration

Life (Basel) · PMID 42942299 · doi

事实｜综述：生长因子与小分子能启动软骨成基质但需时空控制；注射让结构软骨再生仍无临床证据。

点评｜近期能落地的是给现有移植物做生物增强、修补小缺损；把“打针长软骨”当项目卖，现在没有依据。

This narrative review finds that growth factors and small molecules can initiate cartilage matrix formation but require spatial and temporal control, and no clinical evidence yet supports injection-only regeneration of structural nasal cartilage. Near-term realistic uses are biologic enhancement of existing grafts and repair of small contained defects, not injectable reshaping sold as an alternative to grafting.
