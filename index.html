鼻尖客观测量新器械

Design and Application of a Novel Device for Nasal Tip Flexibility and Stability

Aesthetic Plastic Surgery · PMID 42993309 · doi

事实｜自制装置测50名女性鼻尖四点，1.5N下位移8.6–11.2mm。

点评｜把鼻尖软硬度变成数字，方向有价值；但只有50例正常人、无术前术后对比，先当基线工具。

A custom device measured four nasal-tip points in 50 young Chinese women under 1.5 N; mean displacement was 8.6–11.2 mm. Quantifying tip flexibility is useful, but 50 normal subjects without before-after surgery data make this a baseline tool, not evidence of clinical benefit.

鼻尖血管瘤儿科

Clinical Outcomes of Nasal Tip Infantile Hemangiomas: A Retrospective Cohort Study

Pediatric Dermatology · PMID 42978517 · doi

事实｜58例鼻尖血管瘤：口服普萘洛尔者3岁时后遗症67.9%，未治者100%。

点评｜早用普萘洛尔，2mg/kg多半够；外用噻吗洛尔别拿来拖延口服治疗。

In 58 infants with nasal-tip infantile hemangioma, propranolol was associated with fewer sequelae at age 3 (67.9% vs 100% untreated); low and high doses performed similarly. Start oral propranolol early at about 2 mg/kg/day, and do not let topical timolol delay it.

鼻整形术后镇痛RCT

Assessing the Effectiveness of Bupivacaine for Managing Pain Post-rhinoplasty: A Triple-blinded Controlled Clinical Trial

Aesthetic Plastic Surgery · PMID 42864553 · doi

事实｜212例鼻整形：布比卡因表面+阻滞，镇痛药更少、恶心呕吐更低、满意度最高。

点评｜II级证据；组合组省镇痛药、少恶心、满意度最高，表面组疼痛评分最低，按需选。

In a triple-blind RCT of 212 patients, combined topical and nerve-block bupivacaine reduced analgesic use and postoperative nausea/vomiting and gave the highest satisfaction. Level II evidence supports combining topical and block bupivacaine; topical alone gave the lowest pain scores but did not reduce analgesic consumption.

拉皮内镜面神经

Endoscopic Facelift with Isolation of the Fronto-Temporal Facial Nerve Branches: A Clinical Study of 300 Cases

Aesthetic Surgery Journal · PMID 42872381 · doi

事实｜300例内镜拉皮，全程游离额颞支：无永久面神经损伤，暂时性额肌无力100%。

点评｜解剖清楚、可复制，代价是人人都暂时抬眉无力；单臂研究证明不了这买卖划算。

In 300 consecutive endoscopic facelifts with circumferential isolation of the fronto-temporal branches, no permanent nerve injury occurred, but all patients had transient frontalis weakness; median recovery was 14 days. The technique is reproducible, but universal temporary brow weakness and the lack of a control group leave the risk-benefit trade-off unproven.

隆胸脐入路系统综述

The Transumbilical Breast Augmentation: Safety and Satisfaction in a Systematic Review and Meta-analysis of 13,210 Breasts

Aesthetic Plastic Surgery · PMID 42993310 · doi

事实｜17项研究、13,210侧乳房：脐入路血肿0.2%、包膜挛缩1.7%、再干预2.3%。

点评｜数字漂亮，但几乎全是单臂回顾、随访短；适合极怕疤的人，不该当默认术式。

A meta-analysis of 17 studies and 13,210 breasts found pooled TUBA rates of hematoma 0.2%, capsular contracture 1.7% and reintervention 2.3%, with high satisfaction but mostly retrospective single-arm data. The complication rates look excellent, but moderate bias and short follow-up make TUBA an option for scar avoidance, not a new default.

隆胸假体长期随访

Five-Year Clinical Performance and Patient-Reported Outcomes of Mentor MemoryGel and MemoryShape Breast Implants (GLOW Study)

Aesthetic Surgery Journal · PMID 42987925 · doi

事实｜5年随访：初次隆胸包膜挛缩MemoryShape 0.31%、Xtra 1.42%、普通圆型5.16%。

点评｜五年数据都算低，但研究没设计头对头，别拿这三个数直接排优劣。

In the GLOW 5-year interim analysis, Baker III/IV capsular contracture rates in primary augmentation were 0.31% for MemoryShape, 1.42% for MemoryGel Xtra and 5.16% for MemoryGel non-Xtra; the study was not designed for direct comparison. All three rates are low, but without a head-to-head design the ranking is descriptive, not a reason to switch implants.
