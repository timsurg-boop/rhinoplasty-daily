鼻整形鼻尖支撑软骨移植东亚鼻

Pilot Study: Preliminary Experience with Dual-strut Tip Support Using Repurposed Cephalic Trim Cartilage in East Asian Rhinoplasty

Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery – Global Open · Morello AM · PMID 43032422 · doiMSA Surgical Arts, Naha, Okinawa, Japan

事实｜回顾性 10 例东亚初次鼻整形（日本女性，平均 33.7 岁），用梯形鼻中隔延伸移植物加双侧鼻小柱支撑杆，支撑杆取自已切除的鼻翼软骨头侧缘；随访 10.1 个月，无感染、移植物外露、鼻中隔不稳或鼻尖变形。

点评｜创新点：把本该丢弃的头侧缘软骨改造成双支撑杆，不额外取耳或肋软骨就强化鼻尖三脚架。意义：鼻中隔软骨不足、只需中等鼻尖支撑的东亚鼻可少开一处供区；但仅 10 例、无客观测量，属初步可行。

In a retrospective pilot, 10 Japanese women (mean age 33.7 years) underwent primary East Asian rhinoplasty with a trapezoidal septal extension graft plus paired columellar struts fashioned from cephalic-trim cartilage that would otherwise be discarded. The struts were fixed to the distal graft and native medial crura; over a mean 10.1 months (range 7–14) there were no infections, graft extrusions, septal instability, significant tip deformity, external nasal valve compromise or columellar widening. The innovation is reusing excised cephalic cartilage as paired struts, reinforcing the native tripod without a second donor site. It may suit moderate tip repositioning when septal cartilage is limited, but with 10 patients, no objective outcomes and no comparison group, the biomechanical benefit remains theoretical.

鼻整形线雕并发症异物移位

Cephalad Migration of Permanent Thread Fragments Following Nose Augmentation by Thread

Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery – Global Open · Yun CW · PMID 43012028 · doiView Aesthetic Plastic Surgery Clinic, Seoul, Republic of Korea

事实｜1 例 54 岁女性，鼻部线雕隆鼻 5 年后额部不平、眉间硬块；手术取出 8 段移位线（2 蓝 6 透明），nuclear magnetic resonance（核磁共振，NMR）证实为聚丙烯-聚乳酸共聚物。

点评｜创新点：把多年后的线头移位追到具体共聚物材料与皱眉肌、额肌反复收缩的力学，而非笼统异物反应。意义：问诊要追问线材成分和植入年限，眉间、额部不明硬结先想到线头上移，取出按深部异物设计。

A 54-year-old woman presented five years after thread-based nasal augmentation with forehead irregularity and a firm glabellar mass; exploration retrieved eight migrated thread fragments (two blue, six transparent) along the glabella and forehead. Nuclear magnetic resonance identified the material as a polypropylene–polylactic acid copolymer, and repeated procerus and frontalis contraction with unidirectional barbs was thought to drive cephalad migration. The value is tracing a late thread complication to a specific copolymer and to muscle mechanics rather than a generic foreign-body reaction. Ask patients about thread composition and implant age, and treat unexplained glabellar or forehead nodules after nasal threading as possible migrated threads; retrieval needs a deep-foreign-body approach.

隆胸乳房上提假体BREAST-Q

Mailbox Flap Mastopexy: Structural Internal Support Technique to Enhance Lower Pole Stability Breast Ptosis Correction

Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery – Global Open · Centeno-Silva J · PMID 43066456 · doiSensabell, Madrid–Valencia, Spain

事实｜84 例假体隆胸同期乳房上提（2021–2024），平均 39.7 岁，60.7% 为二次上提；4×4 厘米皮腺瓣锚定胸大肌，90.5% 完全矫正下垂，并发症 23.8%，BREAST-Q 从 35.2 升至 93.8 分。

点评｜创新点：用一块 4×4 厘米去上皮皮腺瓣锚到胸大肌做内支撑，把下极稳定从皮肤受力改成结构受力。意义：假体隆胸合并中度下垂、尤其二次上提可少依赖皮肤张力；但并发症 23.8%、无对照，满意度和随访期要如实讲。

In a retrospective cohort of 84 patients having mastopexy with implants between 2021 and 2024 (mean age 39.7 years, mean BMI 23.7), 60.7% were secondary lifts and most had Regnault grade 2 (65.5%) or 3 (31.0%) ptosis. A 4 × 4 cm dermoglandular flap was anchored to the pectoralis with limited subpectoral myotomy; complete ptosis correction was achieved in 90.5%, complications occurred in 23.8% (mainly minor wound dehiscence 5.9%, hypertrophic scar 3.6%, asymmetry 3.6%), and BREAST-Q scores rose from 35.2 to 93.8 (P < 0.001). The innovation is a dermoglandular flap fixed to the pectoralis to support the lower pole structurally instead of relying on skin. It is a reasonable option for moderate ptosis with implants, especially secondary lifts, but the 23.8% complication rate and lack of a control group mean the satisfaction gain and follow-up must be presented honestly.
