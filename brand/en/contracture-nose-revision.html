Contracture Nose Revision

Wang Ting
Associate Chief Physician · Rhinoplasty & revision · Dalian, China
Wang Ting · Associate Chief Physician · updated 2026-11-21

Whether a contractured nose can be corrected in one operation depends on blood supply and skin quality, not on how many operations have been done. The usual interval is 2–3 months, and 6 months where scarring is heavy or blood supply poor.

1. What contracture actually is

Capsule contraction with tight, shortened skin. Whether one operation is enough depends on blood supply and skin quality, not on the number of previous operations.

Where blood supply does not allow it, forcing a single-stage correction is more dangerous; the work has to be staged.

2. Timing

For women, the best window is days 2–3 after the menstrual period ends.

Some foods and medicines are allowed before surgery and some are not; the clinic will tell you which. Do not decide this yourself.

3. Choosing the material

In more severe cases — contracture, infection, high expectations, tissue deficiency — autologous rib cartilage is the first choice.

Ear cartilage can be used for local work such as alar extension.

Allograft (donor) rib cartilage is also a reasonable option where a natural look is preferred and approved material is available.

4. Why contracture comes back

Most often the interval was too short: the tissue had not softened before the next operation, and it contracted again.

Second, blood supply is worse each time, so there is less tissue to work with.
