Rib Cartilage Revision Rhinoplasty

Wang Ting
Associate Chief Physician · Rhinoplasty & revision · Dalian, China

Rib cartilage revision rhinoplasty starts at CNY 30,000–50,000, priced in bands of roughly CNY 5,000 according to how many components are rebuilt. That figure excludes anaesthesia, hospital stay and pre-operative tests.

1. When rib cartilage is the right material

In more severe cases — contracture, infection, high expectations, tissue deficiency — autologous rib cartilage is the first choice.

Ear cartilage suits local work such as alar extension. Where tip soft tissue is thin and blood supply is poor, ear cartilage cannot provide the support needed.

Allograft (donor) rib cartilage is also a reasonable option where a natural look is preferred and approved material is available.

2. Why revision costs more than primary surgery

The cost is in the work, not the material. A revision first has to deal with what the previous operation left behind, then rebuild the framework — two jobs.

A single strut to raise the dorsum and a full framework rebuild differ several-fold in workload, though both are called rib cartilage revision.

Two bands by difficulty: simple (implant removal, minor adjustment) CNY 10,000–40,000; complex (structural rebuild, multiple revisions) CNY 40,000–110,000.

3. Two questions to ask about rib harvest

Is the harvest charged separately?

Is a CT needed, and who pays for it? Both are commonly billed separately and are usually left out of a verbal quote.

4. How long before the next operation

A minor adjustment: 2–3 months. A complex rebuild: 6 months or more.

Operating before the tissue has softened makes contracture likely to recur — the commonest reason repeated revisions fail.
