When can I exercise after rhinoplasty?

Wang Ting · Associate Chief Physician · updated 2026-11-26

Start light activity at 2 weeks and build up gradually. Return to weight training after 1 month, and do not open with heavy loads. Women are advised to avoid the days before their period.
