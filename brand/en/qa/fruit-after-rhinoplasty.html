Which fruit can I eat after surgery?

Wang Ting · Associate Chief Physician · updated 2026-11-27

Room-temperature, soft fruit for the first 24 hours. After 72 hours apples, watermelon, bananas and kiwi are fine in normal amounts. Eat less mango and durian early on.

In the first 24 hours choose fruit at room temperature and on the soft side. Watermelon and apple are both fine, cut into small pieces and eaten slowly. Leave anything too hot or too irritating alone.

After 72 hours there is little to avoid. Apples, watermelon, bananas and kiwi, fruit high in vitamins and fibre, can be eaten normally. Fibre also helps with bowel movement, so you do not have to strain.

Mango and durian are not forbidden. They are high in calories and, in the early days, easy to confuse with swelling or mouth discomfort, so a smaller amount is safer.

Chilled fruit is fine, but do not take big bites straight out of the fridge.

Key facts

First 24 hours: Room-temperature, soft fruit such as watermelon and apple, in small pieces.

After 72 hours: Apples, watermelon, bananas and kiwi in normal amounts.

Eat less: Mango and durian: high in calories, a smaller amount is safer early on.

Chilled fruit: Fine, but not large amounts straight from the fridge.
