How is follow-up arranged after rhinoplasty if I fly home?

Wang Ting · Associate Chief Physician · updated 2026-11-25

Review on the morning after surgery, or the morning after that, between 9 and 11 am. Sutures usually come out in 5–7 days. Only about 10% of returning patients have them removed locally; for a first operation, Dalian is advised.

1. The first review

It is on the morning after surgery, or the morning after that, 9–11 am.

Swelling is easiest to judge at that hour, and any problem can be handled the same day.

The check covers the incisions, the bridge line, breathing and any haematoma.

2. Suture removal

Sutures usually come out 5–7 days after surgery.

About 90% of patients come back to Dalian for it. The other 10% are mostly patients who have had several operations and cannot fit the trip; they have the sutures out locally.

For a first operation, coming back to Dalian is advised. Removal looks simple, but judging how the incision is healing and making early shape adjustments is best done by the surgeon who performed the operation.

3. Remote follow-up from home

Three ways, used in turn:

Video: swelling, symmetry and the look of the incisions, at an agreed time.

Photos: same angle, same light, same distance, so they can be compared over time.

Scales: scores for breathing, pain and satisfaction, to catch what photos do not show.

4. When you have to come back

Fewer than 1% of cases need to be seen in person.

Whether you must return is decided by the doctor from the video and the findings, not guessed by the patient. Not every oddity needs a flight.

The schedule is adjusted to the operation. The written plan given at discharge sets the dates.

Key facts

First review: Morning after surgery or the morning after that, 9–11 am.

Sutures: Out in 5–7 days. About 90% return to Dalian; about 10% have them removed locally.

Remote follow-up: Video, fixed-angle photos and symptom scales.

Return visit: Needed in fewer than 1% of cases; the doctor decides from the video and findings.
