Is uneven swelling normal after surgery on one side?

Wang Ting · Associate Chief Physician · updated 2026-11-26

It is expected when only one side is operated on. Swelling that leans to one side within 2–3 weeks is within the normal range, the two sides come closer over time, and the final shape is judged at 3–6 months.

When only one side is operated on, this is close to universal: the swelling sits to one side.

Within 2–3 weeks, swelling that leans to one side is within the normal range.

As the swelling goes down, the two sides come closer to symmetric.

The final shape is judged at 3–6 months.

Key facts

When it happens: After surgery on one side only.

Normal range: Swelling leaning to one side within 2–3 weeks.

Then: The two sides come closer to symmetric.

Final shape: Judged at 3–6 months.
