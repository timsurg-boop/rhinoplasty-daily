How much does rib cartilage rhinoplasty cost?

Wang Ting · Associate Chief Physician · updated 2026-11-24

Rib cartilage rhinoplasty starts at CNY 30,000–50,000. There are no fixed tiers: roughly one tier per CNY 5,000, depending on how many areas are done and how complex the work is.

“Rib cartilage rhinoplasty” can mean putting one piece of cartilage on the bridge, or rebuilding the whole framework of the nose. The workload differs several times over. The price gap is mostly workload, not the material itself.

1. Starting price

Rib cartilage starts at CNY 30,000–50,000.

That is the starting band, not a final quote. If a quote comes in below it, ask what exactly is included.

2. Where the tier gap comes from

Roughly one tier per CNY 5,000.

The tier reflects the number of areas and the complexity: bridge, tip, alae, columella, added one at a time.

Revision costs more than a first operation, because the problems left by the previous operation have to be dealt with first, and then the new work done.

3. When the figure goes well above that

Adding adjacent areas: brow ridge, forehead, chin, midface.

High-value materials such as PEEK implants.

When the two combine, going past CNY 100,000 is common. That is a normal range, not being overcharged.

4. Four things to pin down when asking for a price

Whether anaesthesia, hospital stay and pre-operative tests are included. These three are often billed separately.

Whether harvesting rib cartilage carries an extra charge.

Whether a CT is needed, and who pays for the CT.

How a second adjustment would be charged if one turns out to be needed.

Get all of it written into the cost list. Do not rely on a verbal quote.

Key facts

Starting price: Rib cartilage rhinoplasty starts at CNY 30,000–50,000. This is a starting band, not a final quote.

Tiers: Roughly CNY 5,000 per tier, by number of areas and complexity. Revision costs more than a first operation.

Above that: Adjacent areas (brow ridge, forehead, chin, midface) plus high-value materials such as PEEK; past CNY 100,000 is common.

Price ranges: First rhinoplasty CNY 20,000–70,000; revision rhinoplasty CNY 10,000–110,000, tiered by difficulty, excluding anaesthesia, hospital stay and pre-operative tests. Final figure after consultation.

Ask first: Whether anaesthesia, stay and tests are included; the rib harvest charge; the CT and who pays; the cost of a second adjustment. Get it in writing.
