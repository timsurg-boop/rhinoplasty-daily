How soon can I fly after rhinoplasty?

Wang Ting · Associate Chief Physician · updated 2026-11-25

Once the sutures are out, usually day 7. Eyelid surgery, which heals faster, can fly at 5–6 days. The hospital provides a fit-to-fly certificate if you need one.

The count starts from suture removal, not from the day of surgery. Different operations remove sutures at slightly different times, so the flight date moves with them.

1. The usual timing

After routine rhinoplasty, day 7, once the sutures are out.

Eyelid surgery, which heals faster, can fly at 5–6 days.

For a specific date, count forward from the suture-removal date of your own operation.

2. When the date moves back

If a problem appears on day 3–4 after surgery, the flight is delayed until things are stable.

That happens in under 1% of cases, but it is not zero, so the review cannot be skipped.

3. Fit-to-fly certificate

The hospital provides one if you need it, issued at the review.

Tell the clinic in advance so you do not have to make a second trip.

Key facts

Flying: Day 7, after suture removal. The count starts from suture removal, not the day of surgery.

Faster procedures: Eyelid surgery: 5–6 days.

Delay: A problem on day 3–4 pushes the date back. Under 1% of cases.

Certificate: Provided by the hospital, issued at the review; ask in advance.
