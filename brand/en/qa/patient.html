What I do

For most cases I use the patient's own tissue: rib cartilage, septal cartilage and ear cartilage. Not because it sounds more advanced, but because when strength and long-term stability are needed, the result is more predictable.

Revision rhinoplasty runs on different logic. First work out why the last operation failed: a structural problem, a material problem, or not enough skin tension. Then decide how to rebuild. Skip that step and change things directly, and most cases need another round.

A second or third revision is harder. There is less cartilage left that can be used, and the scar is stiffer. Whether it can be changed again, and how far, depends on what is actually inside, not on a photo.

What fruit can I eat after surgery?

In the first 24 hours, choose fruit at room temperature and on the soft side. Watermelon and apple are both fine; cut them into small pieces and eat slowly. Leave anything very hot or strongly irritating alone for now.

After 72 hours there is little to avoid. Apples, watermelon, bananas and kiwi, fruit rich in vitamins and fibre, can be eaten as usual, and they help keep the bowels moving so you do not have to strain. Mango and durian are not absolutely forbidden. They are high in calories and easily make you feel overheated inside, and in the early days that is easy to confuse with swelling or mouth discomfort, so a smaller amount is safer. Chilled fruit is fine, but do not eat a lot straight out of the fridge.

Can I drink iced Americano after surgery?

Not in the first 24 hours. At this stage the priority is to let the wound settle and keep bleeding down, so coffee, strong tea and alcohol, things that dilate blood vessels, are best left alone.

After 72 hours, if swelling has not got worse, there is no bleeding and no other instruction, coffee can go back to your usual amount step by step. There is no need to cut it out. Do not drink hard through a straw, and do not drink a large amount in one go. If you feel palpitations, bleed, or swell noticeably more, stop and tell me at your follow-up.

When can I exercise after surgery?

Start gentle activity at 2 weeks. Go back to weight training after 1 month, and do not begin with heavy loads. For women, avoid the days before your period.

How long until it looks natural?

2 months is the peak of swelling, and the nose looks higher and wider. It then settles. From 4–5 months it starts to look natural, and it is about stable at around 2 years.

When can I eat normally after an intraoral incision?

An intraoral incision does not stop you eating normally. Once the anaesthetic has fully worn off and there is no nausea, you can eat the same day. For the first 24 hours keep food warm-cool and soft, take small bites and swallow slowly. After 72 hours go back to a normal diet step by step; there is no need to wait until the stitches come out.

In the first 24 hours the rule is less bleeding: warm-cool, soft, small bites, slow swallowing, and avoid very hot, very hard, spicy food and alcohol. After eating, rinse gently with plain water. Do not rinse hard, and do not keep flushing the wound.

After 72 hours swelling is going down and the diet can return to normal, including beef, lamb and mildly spicy food. Brushing your teeth is fine too, gently, avoiding the incision area. If bleeding continues, there is a clear bad smell, or you have a fever, contact me.

Is uneven swelling normal after one-sided surgery?

After one-sided surgery this almost always happens. Swelling leaning to one side within 2–3 weeks is within the normal range, and the two sides come closer to symmetric. The final shape is judged at 3–6 months.

About cost

Initial rhinoplasty with ear or septal cartilage: CNY 20,000–60,000. Initial rhinoplasty with rib cartilage: CNY 30,000–70,000. Revision rhinoplasty, simple (implant removal or minor shape adjustment): CNY 10,000–40,000. Revision rhinoplasty, complex (structural rebuild or multiple revisions): CNY 40,000–110,000. None of these include anaesthesia, hospital stay or pre-operative tests. Revision is priced by difficulty and the gap is wide; a number can only be fixed after seeing the imaging and the records of previous operations.

I do not quote a starting price, because that number does not help a patient: the lowest price usually belongs to the simplest case and may have nothing to do with yours. The range you get after an assessment is the accurate one. So when someone asks what a full rhinoplasty costs, or what revision rhinoplasty costs, my answer is the same: difficulty first, then the number.
