Is it safe to fly back to China for rhinoplasty?

Wang Ting · Associate Chief Physician · updated 2026-11-22

The question is not “China or abroad”, but three concrete things: whether the records can leave with you, whether there is enough time to recover, and who is responsible if something goes wrong. Flying is allowed 3–5 days after surgery; most patients allow 8 days.

Most of the risk in cross-border surgery is not technical risk but handover risk: assessment in one place, surgery in another, and nobody to go back to when something goes wrong. Handle the handover, and the rest is no different from having surgery locally.

1. Can the records leave with you

Three things have to be in your hand: the operative record, the material used with its brand and lot number, and the pre-operative imaging.

Records you cannot obtain raise the difficulty of the next revision before it has even started.

Ask before surgery: when these will be given, and in what form.

2. Is there enough recovery time

Flying is allowed 3–5 days after surgery; sutures come out on day 7. Most patients allow 8 days; suitable cases can be compressed to 7.

A schedule that is too tight gives up the chance for review and for handling complications.

The acute phase is usually the first 3 days. Do not plan long-haul travel in that window.

3. Who is responsible if something goes wrong

Suture removal is usually at 5–7 days. About 10% of returning patients have sutures removed locally; for a first operation, coming back to Dalian is advised.

Confirm in advance: if infection or a haematoma appears after you return home, is there a local place that can take over, and how is the cost handled.

If this cannot be settled clearly, do not proceed.

4. When not to come back for surgery

A stay shorter than 7 days that cannot be extended.

A condition that needs long-term follow-up, such as poorly controlled diabetes or an immune disease.

Previous surgery abroad with no records, and this operation is a revision: incomplete information multiplies the risk.

5. A simple test

Ask yourself: if something goes wrong on day 4, can I see the operating surgeon within 24 hours?

If yes, it can be done. If no, rearrange the trip.

Key facts

Records: Operative record, material brand and lot number, pre-operative imaging. Ask for them before surgery and confirm the form they are given in.

Timing: Flying 3–5 days after surgery at the earliest; sutures out on day 7. Most patients allow 8 days, suitable cases 7. Acute phase is the first 3 days.
