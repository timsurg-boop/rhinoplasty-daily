How much does revision rhinoplasty cost?

Wang Ting · Associate Chief Physician · updated 2026-11-21

No fixed tiers. Rib cartilage revision starts at CNY 30,000–50,000, roughly one tier per CNY 5,000 depending on how many procedures are involved and how complex the rebuild is.

Two operations can both be called revision rhinoplasty, yet one removes an implant and the other rebuilds the framework of the nose. The workload differs several times over, so the price cannot be a single number.

1. Starting price

Rib cartilage revision starts at CNY 30,000–50,000.

That is the starting band, not a final quote.

2. Where the tiers come from

Roughly one tier per CNY 5,000.

Each tier reflects how many areas are addressed and how much structural rebuilding is needed.

3. When the figure goes well above that

Additional work on the brow ridge, forehead, chin or midface.

High-cost materials such as PEEK implants.

When both apply, going above CNY 100,000 is common.

Key facts

Price ranges: Primary rhinoplasty CNY 20,000–70,000; revision rhinoplasty CNY 10,000–110,000, tiered by difficulty, excluding anaesthesia, hospital stay and pre-operative tests. Final figure is set at consultation.
