Can I drink iced Americano after surgery?

Wang Ting · Associate Chief Physician · updated 2026-11-27

Not in the first 24 hours: coffee, strong tea and alcohol dilate blood vessels. After 72 hours you can go back to your usual amount step by step, and do not drink hard through a straw.

In the first 24 hours, hold off. The priority at this stage is to let the wound settle and keep bleeding down, so coffee, strong tea, alcohol and anything else that dilates blood vessels are best left alone.

After 72 hours, if swelling has not got worse, there is no bleeding and no other instruction, coffee can go back to your usual amount step by step. There is no need to cut it out.

Do not drink hard through a straw, and do not drink a large amount in one go. If you feel palpitations, bleed, or swell noticeably more, stop and tell me at your follow-up.

Key facts

First 24 hours: No coffee, strong tea or alcohol.

After 72 hours: Back to your usual amount step by step, if swelling is not worse and there is no bleeding.

How to drink: No hard sucking through a straw, no large amounts in one go.

Stop and tell me: Palpitations, bleeding, or clearly worse swelling.
