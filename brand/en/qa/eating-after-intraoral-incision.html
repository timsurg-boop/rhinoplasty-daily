When can I eat normally after an intraoral incision?

Wang Ting · Associate Chief Physician · updated 2026-11-27

The incision inside the mouth does not stop you eating. You can eat the same day once the anaesthetic has fully worn off: cool, soft food in small bites for 24 hours, a normal diet after 72 hours. There is no need to wait for suture removal.

For the first 24 hours, work to the principle of less bleeding: cool food, soft food, small bites, swallowed slowly. Keep away from anything too hot, too hard, spicy, or alcohol.

After eating, rinse gently with plain water. Do not rinse hard, and do not keep flushing the wound.

After 72 hours the swelling is going down and you can move back to your normal diet, including beef, lamb and mildly spicy food. Brushing your teeth is fine as well: brush gently and keep away from the incision.

If bleeding continues, there is a noticeable bad smell, or you have a fever, contact me.

Key facts

First 24 hours: Cool, soft food, small bites; no very hot, hard or spicy food, no alcohol.

Rinsing: Plain water, gently; no hard rinsing or flushing the wound.

After 72 hours: Normal diet, including beef, lamb and mildly spicy food; gentle brushing avoiding the incision.

Call the clinic: Persistent bleeding, a noticeable bad smell, or fever.
