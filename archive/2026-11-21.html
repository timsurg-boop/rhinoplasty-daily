鼻整形青少年伦理与指南

Determining the Appropriate Age for Aesthetic Procedures in Minors: A Review of the Guidelines, Outcomes, and Ethical Considerations

Aesthetic Surgery Journal · Gimenez Lynch C · PMID 43053604 · doiHerbert Wertheim College of Medicine, Florida International University, Miami, FL, USA

事实｜系统综述青少年医美指南与实践：覆盖注射、激光、皮肤护理，以及耳整形、鼻整形、缩乳、隆胸等手术；各国最低年龄推荐差异很大，多数项目没有统一门槛；纳入研究普遍报告筛选合适者安全性好、满意度高。

点评｜创新点：把指南、结局与伦理放进同一篇比较，明确“多数手术没有标准年龄”是现状而非疏漏。意义：接诊未成年人时，别把家长签字当合格线，要单独评估心理成熟度、动机与对风险的理解，把“能做”和“该做”分开说。

This review synthesises regulatory guidance, safety outcomes and ethical principles for aesthetic procedures in minors, covering injectables, lasers and skin care as well as otoplasty, rhinoplasty, breast reduction and breast augmentation. Minimum age recommendations vary widely between countries and procedures, and most operations have no standard age threshold. Included studies generally report favourable safety, high satisfaction and psychosocial improvement in appropriately selected adolescents. Its contribution is putting guidelines, outcomes and ethics side by side, making clear that the absence of a standard age is the current state rather than an oversight. For adolescent rhinoplasty, parental consent is a legal floor, not a fitness test; maturity, motivation and comprehension of risk should decide, and 'can be done' must be separated from 'should be done'.

鼻整形鼻中隔翻修

Does Previous Septoplasty Affect Rhinoplasty Outcomes? A Comparative Analysis of Functional, Psychological and Self-Assessment Measures

Journal of Clinical Medicine · Jadczak M · PMID 42941334 · doiDepartment of Otolaryngology with Division of Cranio-Maxillo-Facial Surgery, Military Institute of Medicine-National Research Institute, 04-141 Warsaw, Poland

事实｜75 例鼻中隔鼻整形前瞻队列，27 例（36%）既往有鼻中隔手术、48 例初诊；术后 1、6 个月测 ROE、SCHNOS、NOSE。既往手术组 1 个月总体满意度更低、1 与 6 个月外观评分更差；两组鼻塞均改善。

点评｜创新点：把“做过鼻中隔还能不能做鼻整形”从经验判断变成带量表的前瞻对比，且把外观与功能分开看。意义：二次鼻中隔患者术前要单独交代外观恢复更慢、早期满意度偏低，但不因此劝退；无未手术对照组，只能算术前术后变化。

In a prospective cohort of 75 rhinoseptoplasty patients, 27 (36%) had prior septoplasty and 48 were primary cases; ROE, SCHNOS and NOSE were measured before surgery and at 1 and 6 months, with quality of life and self-esteem also assessed. Prior septoplasty was associated with lower overall satisfaction at 1 month and worse cosmetic scores at 1 and 6 months, while nasal obstruction, quality of life and self-esteem improved in both groups. It turns 'can you operate after a previous septoplasty' into a measured prospective comparison and separates appearance from function. For these patients, consent should state slower cosmetic recovery and lower early satisfaction without discouraging surgery; with no unoperated control group, the changes are observed pre- to postoperative differences rather than proof of causation.

隆胸假体表面系统综述

Impact of Implant Surface Texture on Complication Rates in Primary Breast Augmentation: A Systematic Review and Meta-analysis

Aesthetic Plastic Surgery · Neves C · PMID 43049881 · doiFaculty of Medicine, University of Porto, Al. Prof. Hernâni Monteiro, 4200-319, Porto, Portugal. carolinabrandaoneves@gmail.com

事实｜12 项研究、17,726 例初次隆胸的 meta 分析：毛面假体下移更少（RR 0.10，95%CI 0.03–0.33），包膜挛缩、感染、血清肿、再手术无差异；胸肌下放置感染率更低；期内无 BIA-ALCL 病例。

点评｜创新点：把“毛面防下移、光面更安全”两边说法放到近 1.8 万例里对齐，毛面只在机械稳定上赢，其余并发症没差别。意义：选假体按解剖条件与放置层次定，腺体前放置要把感染风险讲清；零例 ALCL 只是随访期短，不能当免责。

Twelve studies covering 17,726 primary augmentations were pooled. Textured implants significantly reduced bottoming-out (RR 0.10; 95% CI 0.03-0.33), while capsular contracture, infection, seroma and reoperation showed no significant difference; subpectoral placement had lower infection than prepectoral, and no BIA-ALCL case occurred during follow-up. It lines up both camps' claims across nearly 18,000 patients: texture wins only on mechanical stability, with no difference in other complications. Implant choice should follow anatomy and plane rather than market fashion, prepectoral placement needs an explicit infection discussion, and zero ALCL cases reflect short follow-up, not absolution.
