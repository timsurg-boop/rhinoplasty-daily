鼻整形鼻中隔软骨耳软骨肋软骨

Do septal, conchal and costal cartilage grafts differ in functional, aesthetic and complication outcomes after septorhinoplasty? A systematic review (systematic review)

The Journal of Laryngology and Otology · Handoko RE · PMID 43095770 · doiOtorhinolaryngology-Head and Neck Surgery, Universitas Brawijaya, Indonesia

事实｜系统综述纳入 2015 至 2025 年 13 项对照研究、共 3609 例成人鼻中隔鼻整形。肋软骨支撑更稳、远期吸收少于耳软骨，但供区并发症更多；耳软骨与鼻中隔软骨的中期鼻尖稳定性和外观相当。

点评｜创新点：把三种软骨放进同一套功能、外观与并发症框架比较，3609 例在同类综述里样本不小。意义：大重建用肋软骨，轻矫正用耳软骨少取供区，鼻中隔够用就优先鼻中隔；但纳入研究异质性大，不能当统一标准。

This systematic review pooled 13 comparative studies published between 2015 and 2025, covering 3,609 adults who underwent septorhinoplasty. Costal cartilage gave more stable structural support and less long-term resorption than conchal cartilage, at the cost of more donor-site morbidity, while conchal and septal grafts showed comparable mid-term tip stability and aesthetic results. Its value is a single functional, aesthetic and complication framework for all three graft types across a relatively large evidence base. In practice it supports costal cartilage for major reconstruction, conchal cartilage for lower-demand correction, and septum first when adequate, but the included studies are heterogeneous and should not be read as a fixed protocol.

鼻整形增强现实虚拟现实手术导航

Augmented and Virtual Reality in Facial Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery: A Narrative Review

Facial Plastic Surgery · Reddy P · PMID 43078468 · doiDepartment of Otolaryngology-Head and Neck Surgery, Emory University School of Medicine, Atlanta, GA, USA

事实｜叙述性综述梳理增强现实（augmented reality，AR）与虚拟现实（virtual reality，VR）在面部整形重建的进展：AR 导航配准误差已小于 2 毫米，主要用于颅颌面骨、游离皮瓣和鼻整形，软组织仍偏早。

点评｜创新点：按骨性与软组织分开评估成熟度，并点出比较研究缺口。意义：目前可指望 AR 做骨性导航和皮瓣定位，鼻尖软组织判断仍靠手感；成本高、缺对照，短期内进不了常规门诊。

This narrative review maps augmented reality (AR) and virtual reality (VR) across facial plastic and reconstructive surgery. AR navigation platforms now report sub-2-mm registration accuracy and are used mainly for rigid bony anatomy, craniomaxillofacial trauma, free flap harvest and rhinoplasty, whereas soft-tissue applications remain early. The review separates maturity by tissue type rather than treating extended reality as one technology. It suggests AR can currently support bony navigation and flap planning, but not intraoperative judgment of nasal tip soft tissue; comparative data, soft-tissue registration and cost remain barriers.

颅骨修复PEEK伤口并发症植入物

Management Strategies and Clinical Outcomes of Wound Complications after Polyetheretherketone Cranioplasty: A Real-World Single-Center Cohort Study

Research Square (preprint) · Zeng L · PMID · doi

事实｜回顾 580 例聚醚醚酮（polyetheretherketone，PEEK）颅骨修补，138 例（23.8%）出现伤口并发症。保守成功率 46.9%，清创 71.4%，皮瓣覆盖 83.3%，取出植入物 0%；中位愈合 57、40、30 天。

点评｜创新点：用 580 例真实世界数据把四种处理方式的结局并列，样本大于多数同类报告。意义：伤口裂开别只靠换药硬等，尽早清创或皮瓣覆盖更能保住植入物；但属单中心预印本，结论待正式发表复核。

In a single-center retrospective cohort of 580 adults undergoing delayed polyetheretherketone (PEEK) cranioplasty, wound complications occurred in 138 patients (23.8%). Success rates were 46.9% with conservative care, 71.4% with debridement and 83.3% with flap coverage, versus 0% with implant removal alone; median healing times were 57, 40 and 30 days respectively. The main contribution is a large real-world comparison of four management strategies, with flap coverage preserving the implant most often. The practical message is to escalate early to debridement or flap coverage rather than prolonged conservative dressing; however, this is a single-center preprint and needs peer-reviewed confirmation.
