From Surface to Framework: Feasibility of Paired Three-Dimensional Nasal Morphometry in Open Rhinoplasty

Facial Plastic Surgery · Farrokhian N · PMID 43085748 · doiDepartment of Otolaryngology-Head and Neck Surgery, University of Kansas School of Medicine, Kansas City, USA

事实｜前瞻队列 19 例开放鼻整形患者（平均 38.8 岁），术中用手机 Polycam 拍外鼻和暴露的下外侧软骨，生成三维点云。XGBoost 模型判断软骨头侧朝向的准确率 0.895（95%CI 0.669–0.987），AUC 0.911。

点评｜创新点：把外鼻和术中软骨配成同一套手机三维点云，属概念验证。意义：术前手机扫描有望提示鼻尖软骨朝向和支撑，但 19 例、单中心，现在只是研究工具，进不了门诊决策。

In a prospective cohort, 19 patients (mean age 38.8 years, SD 14.2) undergoing open rhinoplasty had the external nose photographed with the Polycam smartphone app and the exposed lower lateral cartilage scanned intraoperatively, producing paired three-dimensional point clouds. An XGBoost model predicted the cephalic orientation of the lower lateral cartilage with an accuracy of 0.895 (95% CI 0.669-0.987) and an ROC AUC of 0.911 (95% CI 0.744-1.000). The contribution is a paired external-and-intraoperative 3D dataset from living patients, captured with a consumer phone rather than a dedicated scanner, and a first machine-learning test of whether surface shape carries information about cartilage orientation. It points toward scan-based planning and objective outcome tracking, but with 19 patients and no validation cohort it remains a research tool, not a consultation aid.
