鼻整形心理状态翻修风险

Association of Anxiety and Depression with Patient-Reported Outcomes After Functional and Aesthetic Rhinoplasty

Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery · PMID 42879137 · doi

事实｜功能鼻整形490例，焦虑或抑郁者术后NOSE更差（35.3对24.1），翻修OR 2.56；美容组179例无差异。

点评｜功能鼻整形合并焦虑抑郁，术前就要讲清翻修风险高、症状改善打折；美容鼻整形不必因此劝退，满意度没差。

In 490 functional septorhinoplasties, anxiety/depression patients had worse postoperative NOSE scores (35.3 vs 24.1) and higher revision odds (OR 2.56); among 179 aesthetic cases there was no difference. For functional cases, disclose the higher revision rate and smaller symptom gain; anxiety or depression should not disqualify aesthetic candidates, whose satisfaction was unaffected.

鼻整形嗓音保留式

The Effects of Preservation and Structural Rhinoplasty on Voice Parameters

Journal of Voice · PMID 42888129 · doi

事实｜40例女性鼻整形，术后3个月jitter、shimmer下降、HNR升高，两者相同；CPPS均无变化。

点评｜两种术式对嗓音只是细微扰动改善，别拿“术后声音更好”当卖点；CPPS没动，整体音质没有证据支持。

In 40 women, both preservation and structural rhinoplasty lowered jitter and shimmer and raised HNR at 3 months, with no change in CPPS and no meaningful between-technique difference. Only subclinical perturbation changes occurred; do not market voice improvement, since CPPS and overall voice quality did not change.

鼻整形围手术期淤青

Menstrual cycle phase and early periorbital edema and ecchymosis after rhinoplasty: an observational study

Frontiers in Surgery · PMID 42923336 · doi

事实｜72例开鼻整形，术后1、3天眼周水肿淤青卵泡期最低，黄体期与排卵期较高。

点评｜能挑时间的话，把择期鼻整形排在卵泡期（月经结束后一周），淤青会轻些；这是探索性观察，别当铁律。

In 72 women having open rhinoplasty, periorbital edema and ecchymosis on days 1 and 3 were lowest in the estimated follicular phase and higher in luteal and periovulatory phases. Schedule elective rhinoplasty in the follicular week if the patient can choose, while treating this exploratory finding as hypothesis-generating rather than a rule.

拉皮氨甲环酸安全性

Safety Profile of Standardized Tranexamic Acid (TXA) Dosing in Rhytidectomy: A Retrospective Comparative Cohort Study

Aesthetic Surgery Journal · PMID 42836382 · doi

事实｜1036例面颈除皱，局麻药加TXA（均582.6mg）未增加缺血或全身并发症，无永久面神经麻痹。

点评｜局麻里加TXA安全性站得住，可以常规用；但这篇只证明“不更危险”，少出血少淤青的获益要靠别的证据。

In 1,036 face and neck lifts, TXA in local anesthetic (mean 582.6 mg) was not associated with more ischemic or systemic complications; no permanent nerve paralysis occurred. The safety signal is reassuring enough for routine use, but this cohort only rules out added harm; the bleeding and bruising benefit rests on other studies.

隆胸轻度下垂聚氨酯假体

Implant-Compensable Ptosis: A Retrospective Cohort Study Comparing Polyurethane Breast Augmentation Alone Versus Augmentation-mastopexy in Patients with Mild Ptosis

Aesthetic Plastic Surgery · PMID 42897247 · doi

事实｜90例聚氨酯假体，轻度下垂只放假体，24个月BREAST-Q 75.6对77.4（加悬吊），差1.8分。

点评｜轻下垂、不肯做悬吊的，选聚氨酯假体并控制容量≤350cc，可以只放假体；代价是乳头平均下移0.8cm。

In 90 women with polyurethane implants, implant-only augmentation in selected mild ptosis scored 75.6 on BREAST-Q at 24 months versus 77.4 for augmentation-mastopexy, with 36 minutes less operative time. For mild ptosis patients who decline a lift, polyurethane implants at 350 cc or less are a defensible option, accepting about 0.8 cm of nipple descent and unproven non-inferiority.

隆胸清醒手术筋膜平面阻滞

A Next-Generation Approach to Subglandular Breast Augmentation Using B-Lite Implants: The Feather Touch Technique

Aesthetic Plastic Surgery · PMID 42900384 · doi

事实｜66例清醒状态肌下隆胸，多层筋膜平面阻滞全部完成、无改全麻，4例轻度位置问题，无严重并发症。

点评｜清醒隆胸技术上可行，关键在阻滞覆盖到位；但单组66例、IV级证据，别当成全麻的常规替代。

In 66 women, awake subglandular augmentation with a multilayer fascial-plane block was completed without conversion to general anesthesia; four minor positional issues and no major complications. Feasible when the block is thorough, but this is a single-arm level IV series and does not justify replacing general anesthesia as routine.
