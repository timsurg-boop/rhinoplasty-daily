肋软骨术前评估钙化

Evaluation of Costal Cartilage Calcification Using Plain Radiographic Images

Aesthetic Surgery Journal · PMID 42869429 · doi

事实｜183例鼻整形1098段肋软骨，平片钙化检出54.1%；重度钙化高峰在40–49岁。

点评｜术前一张肋骨平片能少一次台上意外；40多岁女性重度钙化风险最高，别只按年龄想当然。

In 183 rhinoplasty patients (1,098 rib segments), plain radiographs detected costal cartilage calcification in 54.1%, with severe calcification peaking at age 40-49 (25.8%). A routine rib series avoids intraoperative surprises; women in their 40s carry the highest risk of severe calcification, not the oldest patients.

鼻整形二次手术患者报告结局

Does Previous Septoplasty Affect Rhinoplasty Outcomes? A Comparative Analysis of Functional, Psychological and Self-Assessment Measures

Journal of Clinical Medicine · PMID 42941334 · doi

事实｜75例鼻中隔鼻整形，27例曾做鼻中隔成形；术后6个月外观满意度更低，功能与生活质量仍改善。

点评｜有鼻中隔手术史的，外观预期要提前压低；功能该做还得做，别因既往史劝退。

Among 75 rhinoseptoplasty patients, 27 (36%) had prior septoplasty and reported lower cosmetic satisfaction at 1 and 6 months, although nasal function and quality of life improved significantly. Lower the aesthetic expectation for prior-septoplasty patients, but do not let surgical history discourage a functionally indicated procedure.

鼻整形软骨再生综述

Beyond Cartilage Grafting in Rhinoplasty: Biopharmaceutical and Biomaterial Strategies for Nasal Cartilage Regeneration

Life · PMID 42942299 · doi

事实｜叙述性综述：鼻软骨再生近期只能增强移植骨、修补小缺损，注射再生还替不了结构支架。

点评｜生长因子、外泌体、可注射水凝胶多在体外或关节数据；先当移植物辅助，别当卖点。

This narrative review concludes that near-term nasal cartilage regeneration can enhance existing grafts and repair small contained defects, but injection-only regeneration cannot replace structural support. Growth factors, extracellular vesicles and injectable hydrogels remain largely in vitro or articular evidence; use them as graft adjuncts, not marketing claims.

拉皮深层除皱矢量固定

Comparative Vector Analysis in Deep Plane Rhytidectomy: Evaluating Combined Horizontal and Oblique SMAS Fixation Strategies in Face and Neck Lifting

Aesthetic Plastic Surgery · PMID 42864533 · doi

事实｜80例深层除皱分4组：面部斜向+颈部水平固定，FACE-Q 88.4、WSRS改善2.4分，最优。

点评｜面颈别用同一个矢量：面斜拉、颈水平收，主观客观都更好；回顾性单中心，先学思路。

In 80 deep-plane facelifts, oblique facial plus horizontal neck fixation scored highest (FACE-Q 88.4; WSRS improvement 2.4 points) versus three other vector combinations. Use different vectors for face and neck; the retrospective single-center design supports the concept, not a definitive standard.

隆胸中国趋势

Trends in Patient Profile and Surgical Preferences for Breast Augmentation in China: A Retrospective Cohort Study Before and After the COVID-19 Era

Aesthetic Plastic Surgery · PMID 42980684 · doi

事实｜682例中国隆胸：疫后更年长、假体更大（262.7对253.2cc）、圆型51.8%，满意度反降。

点评｜客人更懂、更敢花钱，不等于更满意；咨询重点从“做大”转到术后预期和长期形态。

In 682 Chinese augmentation patients, the post-pandemic cohort was older, chose larger (262.7 vs 253.2 cc) and more round implants (51.8% vs 34.9%), yet breast satisfaction declined. Better-informed, higher-spending patients are not more satisfied; shift counseling from size to expectations and long-term shape.

隆胸翻修境外手术

Holidays With Perks: A Registry-based Study Investigating Revision Procedures Among Patients With Breast Implants Inserted Overseas

Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery – Global Open · PMID 42910395 · doi

事实｜30,579例假体翻修中11.7%为境外植入；境外组破裂27.0%、移位24.9%，均高于境内。

点评｜低价旅行隆胸的账最后算在翻修和公立医院上；接诊先问植入地、假体型号和原始记录。

Of 30,579 breast implant revisions, 11.7% involved overseas-inserted devices, with higher rupture (27.0% vs 24.5%) and malposition (24.9% vs 20.5%). Cosmetic tourism shifts costs to revision surgery and public hospitals; ask overseas patients for implant records before planning.
